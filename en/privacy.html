22. General principles

The controller processes personal data only for specified purposes, on an appropriate legal basis and in accordance with the principles of data minimisation and security. For data protection matters, the controller can be contacted at info@iconspirante.eu. The controller is not obliged to designate a data protection officer.

The website contains no form, registration, newsletter sign-up, online shop, analytics or marketing measurement tool (such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel), and it does not use cookies.

33. Contact messages

The website has no contact form; visitors may contact the controller at the e-mail address or phone number provided. Data: name, e-mail address, phone number (if provided), the message and the correspondence data needed to reply. Purpose: handling and answering enquiries. Legal basis: legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for general enquiries (the controller's interest in being able to reply to persons contacting it); Article 6(1)(b) where steps are taken at the data subject's request prior to entering into a specific contract. Retention: 1 year after the enquiry is closed; if a contract results from the enquiry, until the claims arising from it become time-barred (section 6:22 of Hungarian Act V of 2013, as a general rule 5 years); in the event of a legal dispute, for as long as necessary. Providing the data is voluntary; without it, the controller cannot reply.

44. Technical logs and IT security

When the website is opened, the visitor's browser, for technical reasons, transmits the IP address, the time of access, the requested address and browser data to the hosting provider (Cloudflare). Purpose: delivering the website, IT security, detecting misuse, fixing malfunctions. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (the controller's interest in operating the website securely). The controller does not keep access logs of these data (according to Cloudflare, HTTP request logs are not retained by default); Cloudflare provides information on its own security-related processing at https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.

55. Age gate

When opening the website, visitors must enter their date of birth. The date is checked only locally in the browser: it is not sent to the server, not stored (neither in a cookie nor in the browser's storage), and not transmitted to third parties. The age gate therefore does not involve any processing of personal data by the controller.

66. Elements loaded from external services

The website's fonts are loaded together with the website from its own hosting; the browser does not connect to an external font provider for them. The website loads one element from an external provider, the map. In doing so, the visitor's browser connects directly to the provider, which therefore receives the visitor's IP address, the requested address and technical browser data (such as browser type and referring page).

  • Map in the contact section of the footer: OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org). Provider: Hyperknot Software Kft., Hungary, privacy@openfreemap.org. According to the provider, it does not use cookies, does not store IP addresses in its access logs, and deletes error logs after 7 days.

Purpose: consistent display of the website and showing the premises on a map. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (the controller's interest in displaying the website properly). The map is only loaded when the visitor scrolls near the footer.

99. Data subject rights

Data subjects may request information, access, rectification, erasure, restriction and, where applicable, data portability; they may object to processing based on legitimate interest and may withdraw their consent. As a rule, the controller replies within one month, extendable where justified in accordance with Article 12(3) GDPR. Requests may be sent to info@iconspirante.eu.
