22. What is a cookie?

Cookies and similar identifiers (such as the browser's local storage) can store information in the browser or on the device. The GDPR also applies to the processing of personal data connected with their use.

33. The website does not use cookies

The website iconspirante.eu, including its English (/en/) and German (/de/) versions, does not place cookies on the visitor's device and does not use the browser's local storage (localStorage, sessionStorage) to store data. The age gate checks the date of birth entered only in the browser and does not save it, which is why the gate appears again on every visit.

44. Statistics cookies

The website does not use any statistics or traffic measurement tool (such as Google Analytics or Matomo).

55. Marketing and social media technologies

The website does not use any marketing or remarketing tool (such as Meta Pixel or TikTok Pixel) and does not embed social media content. The links to social media pages (Instagram, Facebook) are simple links that open only when clicked, subject to the terms of the page opened.

66. Consent

As the website does not store any information on the visitor's device and does not access it, no consent needs to be requested, and therefore the website has no cookie banner (section 155(4) of Hungarian Act C of 2003). Should the operator introduce cookies or measurement tools that are not strictly necessary in the future, it will activate them only after prior, active consent; in the banner, accepting and rejecting will be equally easy, without pre-ticked options, and this policy will be updated.

77. Cookie list

The website's cookie list is empty: the website does not use cookies. Last check of the list: 19 October 2026, on the live website, by browser measurement (Hungarian, English and German versions, age gate completed, whole page scrolled through).

The website loads two elements from external providers (Google Fonts font, OpenFreeMap map). According to their own information, these providers do not set cookies; however, for technical reasons they do receive the visitor's IP address. Details are given in section 6 of the Privacy policy.

88. Rights and contact

Rights relating to personal data and remedies are described in the Privacy policy. There is no link to open cookie settings, because the website does not use cookies.

99. Legal sources
